Today, I saw a little girl. She was walking around with a smile on her face. She was sharing that smile with whoever she saw and met while passing. One of the passers-by was me as well :D.

So, my readers, here's a smile :) I am sharing with you...

Yes... coming back to the halfway left story -

She also greeted a few old people and the ones who greeted her. And you know what, who greeted her next? -> the dogs! Oops, the doggies 🙂‍↕️ One was brown and the other mixed (black and white). They came running to her. She wasn't afraid instead very, very happiiee (that's my way of expressing happiness when very happy!) She played with them, caressed them and also she was whispering something...As I was at a distance so couldn't understand what she was saying to them. Yep, but what I could understand was - "Tata!", which she said after a few minutes when she headed towards a tree. The tree, actually it was a chafa kind of flower plant. Now, who this girl was? Someone totally unknown? Or who? Well, she was that small child inside me. Yes, this revelation can sound like out of nowhere and odd...
