𐙚 Friendship Day

Starting with Friendship Day – 1st Sunday of August and me running and going to Laxmi (shop for last moment buying bands and rolls…me being very choosy abt those 4-5 bands selection and then roll’s color+whatever written on it), then comin' home (optional if i forget bag), going 1-2 flrs up-down and 1st 3 bands fixed. Then callin’ (nah nah uss time pe calling wasn’t mob calls it was haak marne, ghari jaun bolavne). Fixing a meet-up time and going down with that bag, scissors, thode-faar cut kelele ribbons (to save time), then form and roam in grps and tie ribbons, exchange bands. Ah! I remember there was also a craze of rings (not me included karan that never fit me, ajun pn nahi & li’l I am happpiiiiee abt it). Then whn boys would be playing cricket (mostly), go in betwn, pause their play and tie bands. After tying humans, we used to climb on tree house and BiLeTree (acronym we made and gave to that badaam tree) and tie to their branches. And still some ribbons on it do exist. After Lockdown, the evolution began…Drizzles on Friendship Day, then knocked doors and we just got into the passages with our material. Did all kinda wrist measuring, ribbon cutting preparations while sitting on the stairs. Then there itself tied ribbons, bands, and with phones coming in hands – captured our moments.
