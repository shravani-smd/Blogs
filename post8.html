This one, for August…Thr’ the yrs August has been a month of change, evolution…I lost my every 1st and the most loved one in one of the Augusts, coping up was in Augusts, the surprises and shocks were in Augusts and yet one of most beautiful memories and blessings filled in Augusts.

I never knew I would write for a month…Yup, all months are good, some months I romanticize, some months the Universe and skies romanticize like post-monsoon and Oct skies and some months….(anyway, this can continue later)

August! It has always been something specially different. It brings some other kinda energy, vibes and a lot more. Maybe the sequence of events that have been occurred and occurring since childhood…is the reason. But whatever, August is special for soooo manyyy eventsss.

Definitely, one of the major reasons – It is the start of these beautiful, heartful festivals. Rakshabandhan with khau, gifts and small kinda get-togethers; friendship bands and that tying rituals :D; Gokulaastami with showers; 15 Aug preparations; and and and chaahul of Bappa!!! I want to store my August days somewhere, everything (memory space) and diaries are filled. This place would go right + has space😊 I don’t know why and what makes August exactly different. Different in sense of energy it makes me feel…

In my words it is like – Paarijaat. Like its petals - kindly delicate...Like the fragrance of Paarijaat, August feels sigh of relief, soulful and soothing, u know like how we tenderly close eyes and entirely feel its fragrance...kinda slow down for a moment, breathe... The orange dot – it provides a bit of dynamic energy while the white balances, like fresh but still somewhere tranquil and familiar enough... Maybe before the '🐻 months (just mah way) it kinda sustains energy (wow & this word literally made me think abt sustainable energy 🦦) August drizzles make heart heavy for a moment but still the beauty is known. Anyway, coming again…August memorables

𐙚 Friendship Day

Starting with Friendship Day – 1st Sunday of August and me running and going to Laxmi (shop for last moment buying bands and rolls…me being very choosy abt those 4-5 bands selection and then roll’s color+whatever written on it), then comin' home (optional if i forget bag), going 1-2 flrs up-down and 1st 3 bands fixed. Then callin’ (nah nah uss time pe calling wasn’t mob calls it was haak marne, ghari jaun bolavne). Fixing a meet-up time and going down with that bag, scissors, thode-faar cut kelele ribbons (to save time), then form and roam in grps and tie ribbons, exchange bands. Ah! I remember there was also a craze of rings (not me included karan that never fit me, ajun pn nahi & li’l I am happpiiiiee abt it). Then whn boys would be playing cricket (mostly), go in betwn, pause their play and tie bands. After tying humans, we used to climb on tree house and BiLeTree (acronym we made and gave to that badaam tree) and tie to their branches. And still some ribbons on it do exist. After Lockdown, the evolution began…Drizzles on Friendship Day, then knocked doors and we just got into the passages with our material. Did all kinda wrist measuring, ribbon cutting preparations while sitting on the stairs. Then there itself tied ribbons, bands, and with phones coming in hands – captured our moments.

Gokulaashtami ༗

Gokulaashtami – Almost very year, blessed with the showers…I love this festival’s evolution, how we were once spectators, then tried, practiced and learnt balancing by standing on each other’s back (oh no!!!) not back, shoulders! 😬 plannings and weight balancing, shouts and cheers, the one going on the top, bringing encouragement by – Ganpati Bappa Moryaaa!, pauusss, dance thoda-sa, and then damun-bhaagun having prasad together and relaxing the body.

It began with dadas and a few tais standing on each other’s back/shoulders and us (kiddos) watching them, supporting and kse matki phodaychi (as English absolutely didn’t fit here). Then, they started forming the base level and us standing on 2nd and 3rd layers. Then, entirely all the 3-4 layers (max) only us aani matki pn phodnaar. Further, calling kakus and insisting them for base support. After that, us standing at the base layer, guiding and seeing the nxt gen plan and execute…

Aagmanaachi chaahul ✧˖°

After Gokulaashtami or from mid-Aug itself, Ganpati practice would begin. Kuthun hi aale astil sagle, all would gather on time (thoda-faar maage-pudhe), tied resp. dhols, tashas and the tunes began… As kids some of us started with zhaanj, and then thoda vel dhwaj/dhol/tasha. Initially, dadas taught different-different tunes, then few joined pathaks and we got haat (“thaapi ne suru hoto!”), taal, tandaav (us practicing in bus while coming from school), some tunes invented by the bois and giving them their names (ofc not mentioning tht) 😆, punjabi, railway, dholibaaja, morya, thoke… best – shiv garjanaa and ofc the last but unbeatable – Naashik Dhol! 🤌 (who cares nantr kay hota) + dhol uchalne. The practice and maaring rounds – some called it penguin walk. (Ek drushya samor aale but not good to mention abt anyone) This was the chaahul of Bappa’s coming and still is…Vaadan krtana it is always a feeling least narratable in words. In fact, if u have observed, even if one is tired, and then passes by Pathak and hears the beats of dhol or even tarri of tasha, kahitri aapoaap badalta even if for a moment…

Hmmm…that’s it for other stories of joining, the 1st time dhol, Ganpati, etc. are almost preserved in diary 🫶 Now these too have been added here and happppiiieee to have more memory space. Hushhh... Enjoy reading, readers! Grateful as alwaysss…🤍
