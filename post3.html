Jai Hind!

Namaskar, hi, hello all!


In the previous blog I introduced you all to my 1st day of the NCC journey...And I am happpiiieee (and you know meaning of this spelling i hope :D) to see your wonderful response ❣

This blog is on my NCC FY (First Year).

As I mentioned earlier that NCC is of 3 years. So when you join, you are called 1st year cadets, in short the first years. Then, you have your 2nd year seniors and 3rd year seniors.


I remember the 2nd day as well...So, I reached the gymkhana (a place for reporting near the ground) well, properly dressed - wearing track pants, t-shirt in, with upper (dry fit material), white socks, sport shoes, hair tied well inside the hair net and a hairband over it. No watch, no jewelry, not even small earrings and my nose ring. I removed my nose ring in my NCC FY and then after 3 years only, I wore it again :) Yesss, that's me :) So, coming back to that day, we took 7-10 rounds of that ground or maybe more. The good part rather the best was we all (the group of FY and few SY) were together. Then we did warm-up exercises like stretching, star jumps (too challenging at first but then once you understand or get used to then it's fun 😄), normal exercise...

That day I decided that never will I be ever late for NCC parades and reporting,

reason -> 2-3 cadets were late...and they had their excuses/reasons as well...Sir heard their reasons and just said okay, and then pointed towards a pole at the opposite side of the ground which was quite far and said - shoot! these cadets ran fast, touched that pole and came, then Sir pointed at a tree and said - shoot! these cadets ran again touched the tree and came back... this continued till our exercises ended...and so I decided that I will always be on time rather 10-5 minutes before reporting time. And from that point of time, I continued this practice!


Then, after exercise, we learnt fall-in, fall-out,and, and, and - "right-marker!"(fav! because from that person the troop is formed, and the person needs to say it aloud, so that time I got to know my voice and how loud, clear I can shout, command powerfully) did standing drill...

few SY cadets then went due to some work, so we (FY) thought, now maybe we will have tiffin break but no! Sir ordered - lie down! start crunches with count! I was like what is crunches?! I looked here and there at my buddies (that's a term for the same year NCC cadets), with gestures and eye language they encouraged me to follow them because they knew I don't know 😬 I did that for the 1st first and all were like - Aaj toh 6 pack-abs baan jayenge, also in between, Sir ordered us - pratyekachya naavane ek-ek crunch maaraycha and we all enjoyed it a lot 😁Due to that, I got to know my buddies' names :D So, finally that day we did more than 50 crunches!

After that enjoyed our tiffin break. Then, one more Sir came and he was CC (Cadet Captain) So, both of them taught us the Assam Regiment song. It includes simple dance-like steps, simple lyrics/words, is short and has music which we create from our claps and stamps. It is the best for enhancing energy and enthusiasm. The Sirs said not to shy while performing rather enjoy it fully! I loved it the most! That was about the 2nd day.


I think our First year was short and I was the last enrolled cadet at 17th number in mid Nov probably. Due to Covid, the regular enrollment began last that year and ended also late. In March 2021, the TYs passed their C certs. But in the between months they taught us the basic drill, commands, reporting, knots, semaphore, through online lectures taught the SS and CS, they shared their stories when they were in the 1st years, 2nd years, told about our division and unit (Div-3, 3 MNU), our super seniors, seniors, different different camps like NSC, scuba diving camp, EBSB, Yachting, MENU, SSB and a lot more...They built us together as FYs. Another few lessons that I learnt in FY were there is no space for sorry! Instead of sorry what matters the most is one's sincerity, honesty, hard-working and never giving up/learning attitude, dedication, also smartness but with politeness.


To end and slightly make the mood light, here's one of my favorite episode from FY -

So, it was Jan and we were practicing and doing exercises...Sir ordered to do front roll. I remembered my childhood days when I used to do that...This time I was going to do that on ground or road something (yeah...road, ground, rough surfaces are normal for NCC cadets to do exercises and ragda 😌) I was trying but wasn't able to push myself in the forward direction at any cost...my SW buddies tried to push me but no, Sir was trying to help me (male as well as female Sr we address them as Sir) but all in vain. Then, CC Sir told me to practice it on bed at home. I said - Yes Sir!

That day itself, in the evening, I was trying it on bed. Now, at our home we have 1 bed in the master bedroom 2ft 2in above from the floor. I was filled with josh and like - I will do it, I can, Let's do the front roll! I attempted for the 1st time and I was successful, I gained some confidence 🙌 Then again got back to the position and attempted again but what happened this time - I was slightly in a cross direction and when tilted crossly, I ended up falling down from that 2ft 2in bed on ground...Dhapak!!!😬

I was sitting in that position until I had digested the whole scene 😆 While trying to stand, I realized that my right leg's little toe got bent. It was hurting while trying to walk, it swelled. So, immediately we went to Dr. He said it is not even moving with ease, and to put plaster or anything is difficult. He just said to take rest and asked how it happened. When I narrated the incident he laughed and said okay but take rest and don't attempt it again for a few days. He tied that little toe with the 4th toe for support.

Then, when after a day, I went for parade, CC Sir asked me what and how this happened looking at my sandals. So, I explained him that I fell from the bed as he had advised to try front roll on bed. He laughed at my innocent enthusiasm. And then told next time do this parakram by putting a blanket or mattress on floor 😌 Later on, I was successful in doing the front roll and even showed him...And this memory will always stay with me because till today, that little toe finger is in the same way, it doesn't hurt or trouble me now but can't be bent easily but all is good :)

To give farewell to our TYs, we (FYs and SYs) were planning performances. During that time, I got to know my buddies more well and our bond started building strong.

I always cherish those memories.


Major taking - Never shy, just enjoy to your fullest!

Do hear and watch videos of the Assam Regiment song, just to know more and feel it!


Jai Hind!
