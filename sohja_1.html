Suddenly, it was 2026 March, BE in its final stages, overwhelming things, routines, busy-busy like a bee still needing to keep energy (even if fed up), company, college, random mixed overwhelming emotions and so many stuff in mind like the peace was entirely disturbed or literally lost!

After a point, it became so EXTREME that I felt like escaping into some different world where none knows me and where I MUST BE – only ME and with me. Somewhere, at a shaaant place. Somewhere, where the nature will hold me, embrace me, listen to everything and calm me down. Somewhere, where the point will be – no internet connection, only REAL, TRUE CONNECTION. 🌿 Somewhere totally lost in order to meet myself again…

That pt. of time made me realize – Now! Now is the time when I can and have to travel by my own. Literally, like literally just go somewhere. All I needed was – me.

The ‘plan’ started with Triund trek, then Dharmashala, then some random places in/near Uttarakhand…Now, one might think why North? That too for 1st time solo? + that’s pretty far from where I live…The reason – Laaammbachhh jayche hote! 🐻‍❄️

And then permission? -> initially had got but then I asked a few and those few not only stepped back but also, I had to re-think and change destination. (not blaming but thenks becoz that actually turned out things best)

It was around last week of March (not even week, just last 2-3 days of Mar). The ONLY thing in my mind fixed was – I WANT TO GO EKTI TO THE NORTH. THAT’S IT!

At the same time, I got introduced to one condition – you have to take with urself atleast one person. Damn! And who is gonna come with me in sucha short time?!

Clg. frds- nope (they have their reasons), Soc. – nope (exams stuff), sch. – asked but nope -> it’s too lamb :)

Rarely one can find such crazy people who’ll just get up and agree kinda in no time.

The last person with the least hope I looked upto was – my chotu.

At night, with almost no hope, least energy and training mind, I txted him – I want to go somewhere, explained him the story in short, and asked if he would like accompany…

He in no time was – yes, chal jau. My face literally shone up, eyes sparked in those late-night hours.✨🥹

He didn’t even ask me about specific location and days! I asked him 2-3 times! Asked abt his permission! He made sure, he was confirmed in till the next morning! I mean…ahh…my chotu bhau! 🤜🤛

I was surprised unable to find a SINGLE place in my fav region! And I was in no mood to come down (below Northern India, somewhere near, etc etc). I asked chotu to just give me a few more hours to find a place.

Btw, chotu is actually lambu than me. 😁 I just don’t wanna reveal names (iykyk :D) Just ki chotu is chotu with a definitely not so chotu ht.

Then why not dive into that and actually select!

The fear was – these places are still unknown to many, range issues, least idea of transport and roads, less people….but then, wasn’t this that I really wanted… So, if this is it and now if it is coming in front then why not?

One only feels to be feared when a few things are new, but everyone is capable to attempt that new and get familiar to it.
