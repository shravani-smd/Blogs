Namaskar and heyllo readers!

For a few readers this blog can help them for their interviews as well. (Maybe a bit for Qs. tho they are common enough :) )

Today this Miss is sharing one story and thought of hers related to achievements/&success. Because recently she received title of – the best outgoing student.

As much as people know, she is someone who is more drawn towards experiences and learnings. More than looking back at the path she travelled/&will travel, prefers to enjoy and focus on the path she travels. Umm…let’s not go deep into this. For this, I will write some other day :D

This is the award story. I had not thought of going there. The message on popped in the previous evening hours. I saw and didn’t think much. Well, the focus and priority were - next morning’s practical and oral. Engineers reading this know how one prepares for it a night prior ;)

In the morning, again the same msg came on 2 different groups, still I wasn’t thinking for the interview. In the morning reached college, went to the practical lab. Practical and oral went good even though the external was tooooo goooood :) (yes, u got that) Mood was okayish and now had some time to think about the notification. It was 12 noon and the interview was scheduled at 4 pm. I decided to go home because the mind req rest that oral. I had lunch aani mg Punekar mhantla ki duparcha plan paramparenusar tharla asto :D (atleast when one needs it the most)

That time I gave a thought. As I closed my eyes, the 3rd year cadet and 1st year engineering student came in front of my eyes. I felt and remembered that day – the best cadet award, the official one. Even though she was considered and recognized as the best cadet by CO sir, her buddies, her division officials, she was unable to officially go there and receive it. It was this 1st year Engg. lect/practical/exam that she gave her 1st preference to, by keeping a stone on her heart. Never again thought if that was a right/wrong decision but it stays always. Never was she ever behind any awards, yet this Best Cdt was different than all. She never believed in chasing achievements, but this… she herself knew it was hers. But…so was the decision, the heart-wrenching one (karan tila mahiti hota tine NCC kasa complete kelay and what it means) From this, and later interactions she understood – some achievements are beyond awards. She got that in her mind. From that day she again continued to perform the way she did yet more strongly and not thinking about results. She began enjoying the journey such that it would make her think less. With this thought I concluded – Sleep! (it helps 😆 + was much needed). Had a good nap. As I awoke, I checked time casually and it was – 3.50 pm only. I knew, it wasn’t possible for me to reach by exact 4 pm at college and would be late. The late thing – started dragging me into thinking process of ifs-elses (which is a less reliable place to be for me). Realizing that, preferred to take action and started with collecting and knowing what I have achieved from 1st year. It built confidence + surprised me with the best, unique combination that was coming to the table. (I remembered - Charlie Mackesy quoted - "One day you'll see how hard it was and how brave you were."⁠)

Well, this process made me think about – Achievements. I listed the things, and took its hardcopy. In semi-formals, left home. While riding, all I could think of was Cdt. This time I made sure she won’t be disappointed. And so decided to only show up without expecting anything. The only thing that was in my hand – to go there, speak and experience. I reached college at about 4.40-45 pm. I had called and informed Sir. Jogging, running I reached in the lab where about already a few students were present. One of the knowns asked me to enter my name in the list. I wrote and checked my number to be 10. (cricket aathavla :D and smiled)

Hmm… then while talking to a friend I understood I need documents, certificates for proof. So, without a second thought immediately ran towards Xerox centre. I was like if we have come this far and also have the material with us then why to let it go out of laziness, let’s just move and give our best effort. At Xerox center, it took about 15 mins. I knew dept closes at 5.30 pm and the faculty starts leaving about 15 mins prior. I looked at time – 5.09 pm. I literally ran, and could remember the cadet days…😄 Actually, the one who was doing this all was – Cadet Shravani herself. The best cadet. At the door of the lab, the last student’s interview was done and he came out, saw me, and told Sir – yes, she is here Sir. Thank you dude! I went inside. There were 3 professors – one who had once taught our class and knew me, one who had heard of me, and the third mixed opinionated maybe.

I knew I was late, like literally 11th hour prepared (proper Engg. zale asa vatla for a moment) which they pointed. I didn’t want to explain any personal reasons. And all I could honestly do was agree. In almost all the interviews, I believe one should remain humble and honest. It isn’t impressing but more about expressing. I confide that is what makes the difference. (NCC learning it is…) Their 1 Q. – tell us about your achievements. I started from FE – topper, project lead, new learnings, NCC cert., taking parades, etc. then they paused and asked more about achievements in E&TC dept. I told about technical workshops that introduced microcontrollers, VLSI, pcb designing where we developed one. They stopped again asked abt project related achievements. I was like yes, have participated in SIH with a cross-dept team. They asked if achieved any rank – so, I was like no. Then they asked - tell about achievements with numbers. This is where my mind took a pause. But then I continued and told them about ranked achievements in YUKTI, F.E., Evolve, dept. prj. Competition. And then as they going to stop, I took a chance and continued with non-tech. Because for me – the non-tech. -> Dance and Arts have played a vital role in Engg. years and surely continue. They wanted numbers – I had in Pune festival. (slightly shifting – it was a dream come true to perform Shiv Stuti and it turned into reality due to Kathak, my Guru and Visharad girls… That time as well, we just focused on performing our best). Then, one of the sirs asked – what do you enjoy dance or engineering? I told – I did both. Dance is from before I opted for Engg. (Actually, after the interview, I got answer – If Engg is mind then, Dance is heart and for living both are necessary) Then they asked if part of any club or organised events – Till F.E. – NCC, conducted parades, then was a part of IIC in S.E./T.E. and helped with oragnising visits, and have volunteered in events. The last Q. again was – any more ranks or going to be topper of BE? And honestly, I said – No sir. He was like why and who is then? I answered – I am into managing few other things as well and so…(also I least like to be called as a topper, I just study, can be said sincere/studious yet that too, for a certain amt of time only…but not to top🫠) To his next Q. I answered with the topper’s first name because she’s a good friend. He asked if you both compete or have strong competition feelings and I literally smiled and was like – no sir, nothing like that. She is absolutely great and we both really share a good friendly bond. And help. He cleared later that he got different answer for me than other students. And I just smiled :D Then at the end, told about the recent achievement of publishing 1st book. And that’s how ended. But again, the ending Q. – Any more achievements specifically wherein you are ranked 1/2/3? And I was like – No sir! This much only. There the interview ended and at about 5.28 pm I was in the parking.

Well, I was happiiieee because this time, took efforts, didn’t disappoint the cadet and showed up there successfully. That itself was success for me. During the process also realised that I had given my best, did my best and am best for it, even if whoever gets the award. I knew the difference. The results, award, decision making aren’t in my hands. I did what I could do the best.

The other thing which was troubling me was the continuous Q – Achievements and numbers and ranks… Are achievements only counted in form of ranks and numbers?! Why? It can be one of the ways…but there are so many people, everyone has gone through different situations, backgrounds, experiences…How can one merely judge someone’s achievements based on numbers? What is an achievement? When do one says – I achieved…? What is the meaning of – to achieve? I know many Qs. but yup :) it is was it is. These Qs crossed my mind.

I firmly believe – Achievements are more than mere numbers and counts. For someone who’s been doing something from a long time and then finally that completion – is an achievement. For someone who is managing multiple tasks even with a smile and trying to keep it easy – is an achievement. For someone who was unsure about a path and then finally gets it right – is an achievement. For someone who was initially afraid to choose a different path, but chose – is an achievement. For someone passing any exam, of course being a topper, clearing backlogs – these can also be achievements for people. For someone to facing their fear, conquering challenges – is an achievement. For someone to be called/recognized as a good human being – is an achievement.

During the farewell, when the name for Best Outgoing Student was called out, felt overwhelmed by many mixed emotions – humbled for recognition of efforts (which was unexpected), proud for how this journey had begun and how it’s final chapters are, respectful and grateful towards all the teachers, remembering the challenging days, projects, parents and family, Kathak and Ma’am, felt inspiring and good…and lastly remembered very imp – chest up, chin up look up! (NCC 🫡) It was all done for Cdt Shravani and she was there to receive it…

With more fighting spirit yet warm smile,
