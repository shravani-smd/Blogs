this bus does not reach Shoja direct! (je amhala aadhi pasun mahiti hota but tht time...officially opened 🙄) Somewhere in betwn we were supposed to change vehicle/get in cab and that cab would drop us at Zostel.
No idea where to get down, where, what is Aut? How will we be getting a cab there? Payment? Driver? etc. etc. etc. All these Qs can’t be said out loud in front, when we are sitting at home for final booking.
I was just making eye contacts with chotu and his calm attitude – Haan, hoil g… was 🤌 He literally kept me hopeful that yup, we will get there right. EVERY SINGLE TIME, whenever booking and my Qs popped up, I used to look at him and he reassured with 😌

Here, I would slightly like to address one thing to my readers — not always does a girl (your sis, friend, mother or anybody) need reassurance. Yup, she can handle things and she trusts herself too. She isn't getting weak, but just a bit shaky, even with all that strength.
But whenever she does need it, it matters (it really does). Just giving her that reassurance, that little confidence and trust, stabilizes her.
Just a simple, small thing (for a few, it might be) but it MAKES ALL THE DIFFERENCE. Not to take over, not to solve everything for her — just to say, “We're here & we'll figure it out.” Sometimes, that's the only thing she needs the most (with/without words) 😊
