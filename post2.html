NCC - National Cadet Corps

A youth organisation with the motto - Unity and Discipline.

The youth is given a similar physical training like in Defence academies, like exercise, obstacle training/running, sports, parade, RAGDA(sooner or later I will put light on this but iykyk), etc. This training develops leadership, team spirit, self confidence, commanding, self discipline and also the whole 3 years of NCC inculcate the OLQ (Officer Like Qualities). A cadet understands, knows and realises the importance of time management, orders to follow, decision making and punctuality and much more.

Who can join NCC?

Ans. i) Students in classes 8th to 10th are eligible for the Junior Division/Wing (for A certificate)

ii) Students in classes 11th to 12th or the first year of a degree course can join the Senior Division/Wing. (for B and C certificate) I joined in 11th std.

For more information and a will to join NCC, my dear readers, you all can search on the internet.


Coming to my story of the 1st day:

Only my Baba knew about NCC because he had done in his college time in Naval Wing.

NCC was there in my college where I had enrolled for my 11th and 12th studies. But I only got to know about it in brief because of the Enrollment Notice. It was almost at the end when I had enrolled for NCC. I won't go deep in that.

One Sir called me - asked me to come on the ground the next morning at 0630hrs in sports rig. And at that point of time, hardly I knew anything about sports rig, I heard it for the 1st time!!! What? What is it?! Later understood it as a tracksuit...no, actually just track pants and sports shoes. And yup, I had those 😌 What encouraging factor I had to join NCC - Baba+something I wanted to do, by my heart, I chose it...and no one else did that for me...


On the D-day, Aai and I, reached the college ground before time. It was cool, actually cold, I was shivering, no habit of waking up so early and...leave it :) no space for excuses

So, we were waiting at Gymkhana, many old people were walking, doing exercises, also few (maybe) teenagers but older than me, were running, jogging, doing push-ups, etc. Some just exchanged smiles with me. The atmosphere felt warm in that chilled weather. Finally I called and informed that Sir, that I had reached but didn't know whom to contact. So, he said - okay, I'm coming. I had no idea what was going to happen, any test or Q+Ans, or what the Sir will ask, etc. and these thoughts were somehow increasing my heartbeats...Dhak-dhak, dhak-dhak 🫠


That Sir came, he was neither very tall nor like the Sirs in schools/colleges means older ones. Hardly he was just about 4 years older than me. In his hand was a stick-like structure (sabar kro...dheere dheere samjhenge proper terms used for it & if you know already then theek hai) Not only me but my Aai was also surprised to see him as a Sir. Sir asked my mother to drop me there and leave but she said she can wait. He then asked me to step on the ground and glanced at me from head to foot. His expression was not very good. He just asked if I wasn't informed about the tracksuit/dressing. I nodded and said I only understood about track pants and shoes. My hair were tied as a ponytail, I was wearing a cute half-sleeve t-shirt, a watch, track pants and sports shoes. He then asked if I could run, I said - yes! Next Q. - How many rounds of this ground? I said - 2/3 He laughed and ordered to run for 4 rounds...

I was not at all comfortable in running, actually I didn't like to run, maybe I was a slow runner, hardly having any athletes quality at that time. Also was wearing those sports shoes for the 1st time...I also didn't like to wear those shoes for anything... And I was joining NCC. Huh...ata hasu yetay te aathavun 😄


I had no choice but to say - Yes! Without a second thought, I started running with the motive to finish the 4 rounds as fast as possible. I completed the 1st round with having used my 50% of energy. Still I wasn't that tired, continuing the same level of energy I could complete 1.5 rounds and I was coughing and hashh-hushh...then from back another girl came, she started motivating me... And with her I completed my 2nd round. I began calling her "didi", she didn't say anything about that. Talking to her at most I could complete were 2.5 rounds only! And then that didi also went ahead...By simply walking, 1-2-1-2, I said Sir, I finished 3 rounds and I am feeling dizzy and can't even stand. He immediately said to sit and remove shoes. He asked if I had breakfast, I said - No.


I don't think that time Aai was there or able to see me. I immediately fell on ground, I didn't even check if it was clean or anything and removed shoes, socks...that didi gave me water and still, today I can remember and feel that felling-wali feeling (actually sitting down) 😮‍💨 I got up in 5 mins again, he called a SD(boy) and asked him to give his upper (jacket in regular terms) to me, I wore it and as I was trying to pull up the chain, I broke it :) that was veryyyyyy embarassing!!!!Then I thought, now Sir would say something else to do...but, but, but! He said - You didn't complete 4 rounds, only 3 you did...I said - Sorry Sir, and before I could say anything else he said - It is an order, you have to now run 2 more rounds...1 you complete by your own and then join the troop.

I started running again, I saw didi, she asked what happened and I narrated whatever Sir had told, ordered! I asked her where's the troop which Sir mentioned. She pointed to a group of about 20 people who were jogging on that same track but were at the opposite side. The warm rays of sun felt like divine blessings and immediately I completed my 4 rounds but till that time the troop had almost completed half of the round and from a distance Sir was watching me. I ran again and finally matched their pace. I guessed that few faces were those with whom in the morning I had exchanged smiles...tab achha laga That round finished, 2nd round finished and I was like - Ruknaaa kaab haii!!! And they told - Jab tak Sir nahi kehte tab tak no! And Bappa was kind enough as after that round, Sir stopped us...biggg sigh of hushhh


Then he got us arranged in file and rank arrangement. Checked nails, dressing, handkerchief, for boys - haircut and shaving, for girls - a net and a hairband to cover hair, which I wasn't wearing and knowing about. Then that didi took me aside and explained me about the dressing and other rules.

Sir ordered her to teach me standing drill and now, that was the part I learnt quickly and sharply...😌 Didi and Sir, both appreciated. Then we all sat and ate together during the break, got to know each other, buddies hoyla suruwat zali...and later on Sir shared his camp experiences... I enjoyed that time the most because hardly any physical training or exercise happened that time :)

One thing that I still remember what Sir said/advised me for the 3 years - Eat, work hard, hardest with and on your potential on ground, go home, have lunch and sleep and then after nap study whatever you do, etc. And truly it helped! I was the youngest among them all but the best part was they all made me feel comfortable and like one of them from the very first meet and day! That's the speciality and best part of NCC and buddies... 🤗


The very 1st day, I learnt very very very imp lessons to which I stick till date:

1. Never, never, never judge a book by it's cover or by mere external appearance.

2. In the haste of finishing fast, there are often chances to lack! So, be aware of the whole task, start slowly, steadily because even for completion of any task we need energy! Never be in haste!

3. Best greeting - Jai Hind!


This was just my NCC Day 1… many more stories, learnings and memories to share! Stay tuned as I take you further into my NCC journey. My golden years - 2021, 2022, 2023

Jai Hind!
