Growing Up?

Namaskar 🙏🏻, hello 🙌🏻 to the readers!

Okiee! So, this one is as random as that. Basically, this happened today, in the evening as I reached home. Yep, it is a normal thing and etc etc. but initially the feeling was weird. Like kdhi vichar pn nhavta aala ki ashi hi ek vel yeil bhetnyachi…

What happened was I met my childhoodie friend while I was waiting for the lift to come. He is one of those friends ki whenever we meet/even I just look at him, I simply smile, burst out laughing widelyyy 😆 (default setting)(now that’s his talent I would ACCEPT👍)

Actually, this is absolutely usual and casual for us…like from many years/months we have been just meeting at that spot!? Hmm…(karan tithech aapn bhetat aahot yaar even if living -1/2 flrs 🙃 Wooww) (growing up!? :) )

Let’s get back.

I parked my gaadi, and like usual, I was waitin’ for the lift to come. I was checking my phone for some work stuff and messages.

Randomly a person came and stood beside me and I just glimpsed. He was wearing formals - white-blue checked shirt, trousers but messed hair 🙆 :) I srsly kinda ignored!

I got back into work thing but that slightlest facial expression had caught me to look again –

Ag! Heylo!
That expression like – It is me! Where you?

And then, tht felt familiar and I was like –

Demnn! I literally couldn’t recognize u dude in those formals!!! 🤣🙈

He said he had this GREAT chance of surprising – doin’ BHOK! 👻 (srsly u could have! pch…I missed it re)

Laughing at our silly things, we entered the lift, had our hasat-khelat talks for umm…abt max. 30 seconds until the lift reached his flr.

At the end all I said was –

This is kinda was weird/different re.

Like who had thought, one such day/evening time after office hrs would arrive wherein we will be wearing formals, coming damun-bhaagun from resp. works (na ki aadhisarkha khelun), have sucha small yet energy-sharing talks of comfy and routine…those kiddos who used to be totally unbothered…like I had never imagined or even thought abt sucha time.

We never did.

(readers, here I know the sentences are a bit up-down…these are entirely raw…written just as passed)

Today, simply that incident made me look back into memories and past situations like how fast the time runs.

Those days and times to wait near the lift – after playing, talking, parties, etc. is done, time to go home – 9pm (tho this is constant, right? 🐣 iykyk) after coming from school, college…

And this time – after resp. works.

Maybe…I wasn’t ready for this and so…so, all this stuff came here.

Well, somewhere this indicated growing-up? Umm...maybe…

But the best part – Yup, still those few moments and people remained, remain dear with the lift conversations…

Yo! Time is THE thing.


With a growing-up thought, yet a kiddo somewhere,
