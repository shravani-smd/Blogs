The Name Changes, The Game Remains

Namaskar 🙏🏻 hey, helllooo 🙌🏻 to the readers!

Always grateful for y'all response and patience :)

Yup, today just sharing a small incident (you can take it as a story, and yes if you ever noticed and checked your surroundings, I am very sure, y'all will relate 😌

Today after returning from dance class, I sat in the garden as I didn't had home keys (simply forgotten to put in bag) 😬 I was enjoying my swing like always while the kiddos were playing on the slope – Zoop (which as much as I remember, we called it Zooo... / Zoom).

I wasn't watching how they played but could hear every topic and names they were taking and running from one side to the other. A few topics and names suggested really new for me, means I felt – yup, at that age I rarely knew those things (or even our group), especially jra mothe-mothe, accent-wale English names, probably of movies. There I got a feeling that maybe now the time has evolved.

Suddenly, abc's (consider as a name) baba from the 4–5th floor called her – Vr ye! Lagech!

And the response was – ho ho aalech!

Friends – parat nahi yenar ka?

abc – Are yein yein, yenar aahe mi parat! Aalech!

I was a spectator and then suddenly from a surprised expression, got back that smile. And realised, even after years and time-passing, some habits, reactions always will remain as it is.

The language, names, thoughts, actions might change but the game and core-ness is un-changeable. Well, the name changes but the game remains the same...

It is a simple thought, and a day-to-day thing which we miss to recognize and address. While growing up, we lock ourselves in our own space and start thinking – the life is too busy, fast-paced and continuously changing.

Well, agreed to some point that it is true and the situation makes us think and work in that manner.

What matters more is to balance, step out for a moment, time, go outside, exchange smiles / even just observe what is happening around us and forget ourselves for that moment. Observe the simple things that would connect us again to our root-self.

With good memories and a warm smile, 🦢
