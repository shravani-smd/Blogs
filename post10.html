Coming towards nature (this made me think)

As I have written, during Autumn, I have mostly looked at the fall – specially the green leaves that gradually turn orangish, then red, maroonish and eventually drop down after a time. I had always captured how the tree sheds, leaf-fall, those shades like from the time when the tree is full of leaves to that point when hardly on any branch any leaf could be spotted.
This time, I could observe the opposites simultaneously. And now on, this would stay.
I had arrived from class in the afternoon. I was casually looking outside of my window – blue and almost clear sky, pretty bit sunny, few whites just existing… It was a slow afternoon. I wanted to see something different and new (& the best way one can do it is by simply lookin’ at the outside world (sometimes ignoring people)).
After the skies, I saw bul-buls on the wires swinging and then they flew. My eyes then caught the attention of the autumn-fall. Exactly, below my window, there is our garden. It has 2 big-leaf trees (badaamache zaad). Maybe, I have always observed one. The one whom I have described up. This one is in the mid (approx) of the garden. The other one is exactly below my window. I got a view of both that day.
Let’s for understanding readers, call one as G and the other as R. (y’all will get the naming too as I go ahead, have patience)
My attention got caught immediately by R. Almost all its leaves had turned reddish, maroonish, a few dry and ready to drop. I was looking and trying to find any different shade…I remembered – where is the G? Both of them must have turned red, right? So, as I turned my eyes sideways towards mid of the garden, G’s leaf-fall stage had gone. To not witness that, I felt a li’l disappointed and late. But then thought – Zala te zala, zala late. Te tr aapn nehmich pahato…What’s happening now. So, I started observing G. Well, a few branches had fresh green leaves, a few from the top – the li’l new greens were budding, too tender. And as the sunlight was falling on those greens, they looked prettier and shone. I could simply adore the change.
From past so many years I was – even the fall is beautiful kinda person. October/Autumn = look at the fall. This simple view, that moment gave another perspective. Much more meaningful to join and continue.
When I saw both the trees from my window in one visual – I had totally gone entirely into heartful admiration state. R – completely having leaves of a variety of shades and almost ready to drop. G – no old leaf, all shining greens, many in budding state like to begin fresh life again irresp of fall (which have passed). 2 entirely different phases but of the same life. Yet, both necessary.
I might go too deep in this, for it felt that way.
Maybe, that's the thing about fall. It doesn't just show an ending; it carries a hope of freshness, too. The new leaves might not be there yet, the branches might look bare for a while, but the budding does happen in its time. And maybe, that's enough for now – to know that there's something new waiting to grow…🌿
